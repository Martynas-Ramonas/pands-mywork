The evening breeze moved gently over the green fields, where people were gathered beneath several trees. Everyone seemed cheerful as they shared stories, sweet tea, and fresh cheese. Nearby, a stream reflected the clear sky, while bees drifted between flowers. The scene felt peaceful, simple, and complete. Even the sleepy dog beside the fence opened one eye before resting again. Later, three friends decided to leave, yet they agreed to meet here next week for another picnic.
